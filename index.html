A rooftop restaurant and convention hall in Chhagalnaiya

Green Lounge is a rooftop restaurant in Chhagalnaiya, Bangladesh. Come for a meal with family or friends, ask for takeout, or reserve a table before you visit.

The menu runs from Dala platters and Bangla food to biryani, grills, burgers, pizza, pasta, chowmein, seafood, shakes and coffee, with Bangla and English names side by side.

Green Lounge is also a convention hall, for programs, seminars, gatherings and celebrations.
