Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Green Lounge collects when you use this website and how that information is handled.

Last updated: October 2026

Overview

Green Lounge Rooftop Restaurant & Convention Hall (“Green Lounge”, “we”, “us”) operates this website so visitors can view our menu, learn about the restaurant and convention hall, and request a table reservation. We keep data collection to a minimum and only use the details you choose to send us.

Information we collect

The only place on this website where you enter personal information is the reservation form. When you fill it in, it collects:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your preferred reservation date
  • Your preferred reservation time
  • The number of guests
  • An optional message, if you choose to add one

You provide this information yourself. The website does not ask for any other personal details.

How your reservation request is sent

When you submit the reservation form, your details are placed into a pre-written message that opens in WhatsApp. You then press send in WhatsApp to deliver the request to Green Lounge. The information travels to us through WhatsApp so we can handle your reservation request and reply to you.

A submitted request is not a confirmed booking until Green Lounge replies to confirm it.

How we use your information

We use the details you send only to:

  • Understand and process your reservation request
  • Contact you to confirm, adjust or follow up on your request

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

No accounts and no online payments

  • This website does not create user accounts, and you do not need to register or log in to use it.
  • This website does not process online payments. No card or banking details are collected here.

Data storage

This website does not store your reservation details in a database of its own. Your request is sent to us through WhatsApp, and any record of it stays within WhatsApp and on your own device, under the usual messaging between you and the restaurant.

Third-party services

To provide its features, this website relies on a few third-party services. When you use the related part of the site, that service may receive information such as your device’s IP address, in line with its own privacy policy:

  • WhatsApp — used to send and handle reservation requests and to message the restaurant.
  • Google Maps — used to show our location on an embedded map and to open directions.
  • Google Fonts — used to display the website’s fonts.
  • Facebook and Instagram — linked so you can visit our social media pages.

These services are provided by other companies, and their use of any information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for general use by people booking a table or learning about the restaurant. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website or how we handle requests. Any updated version will be posted on this page.
